# 03 — Ship Store Category presentation

**What to build:** Stores can present the shared Category structure in their own POS browse menu by choosing Category visibility and local order, without creating private Categories or changing Product classification, price, status, or scanning behavior.

**Blocked by:** None — can start immediately.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Every Category has one retained Store Category Presentation per Store with local visibility and order, including safe creation and backfill behavior.
- [ ] Organization Category management remains the source of Category identity, structure, global lifecycle, and default order.
- [ ] Store workspace controls affect only that Store's Category browsing, and hidden Categories do not act as a second Product sellability switch.
- [ ] POS category browsing uses the selected Store's presentation while direct Product scan behavior and active Product Offering validation remain unchanged.
- [ ] Service, route, Admin, and POS behavior tests cover local presentation, Organization/Store authorization, and the separation from Product commercial status.
