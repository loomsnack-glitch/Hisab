# 07 — Add Organization and Store reporting scopes

**What to build:** Organization owners can view aggregated, Store-attributed operational reports with Store-by-Store breakdowns and an optional Store filter. A Store workspace shows its own figures only, including clearly labelled customer-balance visibility.

**Blocked by:** 05 — Introduce Store Customer Accounts.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Organization Reports show totals across all Stores and a Store-by-Store breakdown for supported operational measures.
- [ ] An Organization Report can be limited to one Store without changing ownership or permitting cross-Store mutation.
- [ ] A selected Store workspace presents only that Store's reporting data.
- [ ] Customer reporting shows Store Customer Account balances separately and labels any all-Store sum as visibility-only, not a collectible account.
- [ ] Report filtering and aggregation preserve Store attribution for sales, purchases, expenses, and money movements.
