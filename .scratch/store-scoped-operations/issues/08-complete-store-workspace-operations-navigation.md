**Blocked by:** 02 — Manage Store Product Offerings; 04 — Manage Store Vendor Availability and purchase defaults; 06 — Enforce Store-local customer credit collection; 07 — Add Organization and Store reporting scopes.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The Organization workspace retains Catalog Product and Vendor creation, Units, Customers, Stores, and all-Store reports without presenting Store-private creation paths.
- [ ] Navigation state, page titles, filters, and empty states make the active Organization/Store scope clear.
