# Hisab is authoritative for synchronized Google Contact fields

Google Contacts Synchronization is a one-way export: after exactly one normalized phone-number match, Hisab overwrites only the Google Contact's name and matching phone entry while preserving its unrelated fields and additional phone numbers. The linked Google Contact follows later Customer name and phone changes; a Customer without a phone number stops synchronizing and leaves its existing Google Contact unchanged; a phone-number collision or multiple matches becomes a visible sync conflict and changes nothing automatically. Ganatri never deletes a Google Contact, including after disconnect or removal of Customer data. This makes Hisab's Customer data reliable for operational contact details without treating Google Contacts as a competing customer-data source or destroying the client's personal contact information.
