# Refunds and License Revocation are Platform Administrator actions

The initial release has no self-service cancellation or automatic prorated refund. A Platform Administrator refunds through Razorpay and records the corresponding License Revocation in Ganatri Console: a scheduled term ends before it begins, while an active term ends on an explicitly selected date. This preserves a reconcilable payment and access history without coupling entitlement timing to an unchosen refund formula.
